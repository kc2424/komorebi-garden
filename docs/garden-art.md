## 生成プロンプト

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square background artwork for a cozy personal botanical garden mobile app, with interactive plants to be overlaid separately. Create a beautiful original illustrated miniature garden, soft gouache and gently dimensional storybook diorama style, delicate paper grain, warm morning sunlight and dappled shadows, lush spring green, ivory and warm wood palette. Slightly elevated three-quarter overhead view. Essential composition: a large mostly clear grassy planting lawn occupies the central 65 percent of the square from y=25 percent to y=88 percent; it must be calm and unobstructed so collectible plant sprites can be overlaid. Around the periphery only: a tiny sage green glass greenhouse along upper right edge, rounded leafy trees framing upper left, a low wooden fence near the back, a curving narrow cream gravel path skirting left and bottom edges, a little empty terracotta pot and garden bench near a corner. Small distant flower accents at borders only. No sky band, no horizon taking up space: mostly garden viewed from above. Colors light enough for small forest-green labels on overlays but not washed out. Inviting, quiet, lovingly detailed natural shapes. No text, no lettering, no UI controls, no characters, no watermarks, no large central objects or preplanted flowers in the open lawn. Standalone seamless-looking composition with edges fully painted, square 1024x1024.
